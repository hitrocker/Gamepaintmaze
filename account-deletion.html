Delete Your Account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Paint Maze and tap the account icon on the Home screen.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Continue.
  4. Enter the current password for an email account, verify with Google for a Google account, or continue directly for a guest account.
  5. Tap Delete Forever.

What is deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account and user ID.
  • Email association and public display name, where applicable.
  • Easy, Medium, Hard, and Extra Hard leaderboard progress and timestamps.
  • The deleted user's progress cache on the current device.

What remains

Device-wide theme, sound, haptics, camera shake, and paint-splash preferences remain. Firebase or Google may retain limited security logs, backups, or legally required records under their documented retention terms.

Cannot access the app?

Email hitrocker27@gmail.com with the subject Paint Maze account deletion request. Include the sign-in method and enough non-sensitive information to locate and verify the account. Never email a password or Google token.
